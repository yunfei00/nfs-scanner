# Sprint Review Checklist

Use this checklist at sprint review gates.

## Checks

- Is the sprint goal complete?
- Does every task have a commit?
- Does `python -m compileall nfs_scanner` pass?
- Is the old UI still available?
- Has `.ai/daily/` been updated?
- Has `.ai/decision_log.md` been updated when non-major decisions were made?
- Did execution stop after reaching the review gate?
- Are remaining limitations documented?

## Result

Record:

- Sprint summary.
- Commit list.
- Checks and results.
- Known limitations.
- Next recommended sprint or task.
